## Power and menu/bot sound effects

The one-shot clips for the four Days of the God, bad omen, good fortune, the four Wraths, `sfx-card-hover.wav`, `sfx-bot-result.wav`, `sfx-soft-good.wav` and `sfx-soft-bad.wav` (`Assets/_Project/Audio/sfx-*.wav`) are original, procedurally generated by the scripts in `Tools/MusicGen/`. No third-party assets, no license required.

## Music

`music-the-six-faces.wav`, the main theme, is original: composed as MIDI (`Tools/MusicGen/godboard-theme-the-six-faces.mid`, written by `compose_theme.py`) and rendered by `render_theme.py` with procedural instrument models. No third-party samples or assets, no license required.
